SHEET 4 OF 8 — DESIGN REVIEW

Projects

MDAR — Medication Dispensing & Administration Robot

Senior capstone · CAD lead · Onshape · 3D printing · Rapid prototyping · Mechanical assembly

What

  • Architected the mechanical subsystem for a robotic medication-dispensing platform with integrated sensing and embedded control
  • Developed a precision mechanism for single-pill isolation, transport, and dispensing
  • Implemented sensor-based validation using depth sensors, with structured testing across edge cases

How

  • Led detailed CAD design integrating NEMA stepper motors, a gantry system, and a turntable concept
  • Performed tolerance analysis and mechanism design for a threaded-rod gantry and vacuum end-effector for consistent part separation
  • Built and tested physical prototypes, measuring dispense time, noise level, and cycle reliability

Results

  • Near-100% dispensing accuracy across 50+ cycles under repeated operation
  • Identified and mitigated failure modes including jamming and misfeeds through iterative refinement
  • Delivered a mechanically robust, sensor-validated system with consistent, repeatable performance

Personal project · CAD + embedded · ESP32 (Arduino)
