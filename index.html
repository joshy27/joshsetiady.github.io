SHEET 4 OF 8 — DESIGN REVIEW

Projects

MDAR - Medication Delivery Assistive Robotics

Senior capstone · CAD lead · Onshape · 3D printing · Rapid prototyping · Mechanical assembly

Led CAD development within a 5 person capstone team and architected the mechanical subsystem for a healthcare robotic dispensing platform, integrating NEMA stepper motors, capacitive sensors, and embedded controls. Designed and tolerance analyzed a precision pill singularization mechanism using a vertical threaded rod gantry and vacuum end effector with capacitive presence detection, then selected and integrated stepper motor drivers and limit switches and iteratively tuned motion profiles to eliminate mechanical binding across the gantry's full travel range. Validated the system across 50+ dispense cycles at near 100 percent accuracy, mitigating jamming and misfeeds through iterative refinement to deliver a mechanically robust, sensor validated system with consistent, repeatable performance.

Personal project · CAD · Kinematic simulation · Structural analysis

Built and mechanically validated a modular 6 DOF robotic arm with load bearing 3D printed joints, optimizing geometry and infill structure to support 3 to 5 kg payloads while maintaining stiffness to weight efficiency.
